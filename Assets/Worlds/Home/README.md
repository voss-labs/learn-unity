# Home

A cozy living room and kitchen, built twice with the same layout: one with low-poly models and one with realistic ones. A doorway joins them. Press 1 and 2 to jump between them.

Lessons 4 and 5 use this world.

## Why twice

So you can compare. It is the same floor plan and the same lights, and the two rooms feel completely different. It also shows the real trade-off: the stylized room has a full set of matching furniture, while the realistic room had to make do with whatever free models exist, so its fridge and cabinets are plain boxes.

## What to look at

- **The holder trick.** Each piece of furniture is an empty parent with the model as a child. Lesson 4 explains why.
- **The light.** A warm ceiling light, a lamp and a kitchen light over low ambient, and the sun coming through the window. Lesson 5 explains why this reads as cozy.
- **The materials.** `Assets/Worlds/Home/Materials` holds the parquet, plaster, marble and rug.

## What it still needs

- A real fridge and kitchen cabinets in the realistic room. This is the exercise in lesson 4
- Curtains at the windows
- A sound: a fridge hum, a clock ticking
- A TV that turns on when you walk up to it
