# Starter

A courtyard with a few blocks, a ramp and a spinning cube. Lessons 1 to 3 use it, and every new world begins as a copy of it.

## What's here

| In the Hierarchy | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Courtyard | The ground, the walls and the Spinning cube from lesson 1 |
| Collision playground | One object per collision rule, from lesson 3. Delete it in your own world |
| Sun, Global Volume | The light and the final look of the picture, from lesson 5 |
| Spawns | The places the number keys jump to. Add your own with Ctrl+D |
| Player | The shared prefab from `Assets/Core`. Keep it in your world |

Everything is made from plain cubes on purpose, so you can see how little it takes to have a place to walk around.

## Make your own

Use **Learn Unity > New world**, and follow [Add your own world](../../../docs/add-a-world.md).
